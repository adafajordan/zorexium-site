Orders & Shipping

How do I track my order?
After your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also visit Track an Order and enter your order number and email address to view real-time shipping updates.
How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Expedited (2-day) and overnight options are available at checkout depending on your location and the seller's dispatch settings.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
Orders may be canceled within 30 minutes of placement if the seller hasn't yet processed them. Go to Order History in your account, select the order, and click "Cancel Order." Once an order is dispatched, you'll need to start a return.
My package is marked delivered but I haven't received it.
Please check with neighbors or your building mailroom. If the item is still not found after 24 hours, contact our support team with your order number and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
Do you ship internationally?
International shipping availability depends on the individual seller. Filter the marketplace by "Ships Internationally" to find eligible listings. Customs and import duties are the buyer's responsibility.

Still need help with your order?

Our support team is available Mon–Fri, 9 AM–6 PM EST.

Returns & Refunds

Most items sold on Zorexium can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Conditions apply. View full policy.

How do I start a return?
Go to Account → Order History, find the order, and click "Start Return." Select the items to return, choose a reason, and print the prepaid return label. Pack and ship within 7 days.
When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are processed within 2–3 business days of the seller receiving the item. Depending on your payment method, funds may take an additional 3–5 business days to appear in your account.
What items are not returnable?
Software, digital licenses, opened thermal paste, and custom-built PCs are generally non-returnable unless defective. Check the individual listing for seller-specific return policies.
The item I received is damaged or incorrect.
If you received a damaged or incorrect item, contact support immediately. Include your order number, photos of the damage or mismatch, and we'll prioritize a resolution under the Marketplace Guarantee.

Account & Security

How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot account?" on the login page or visit Login/Register and use the "Forgot Password" link. You'll receive a reset link to your registered email within a few minutes.
How do I enable two-factor authentication?
Go to Account → Security Settings. Enable 2FA with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator or Authy) for the best protection. We also support SMS 2FA.
How do I change my email address?
Visit My Profile → Edit Profile. You'll need to verify both your old and new email addresses before the change takes effect.
I think my account was compromised. What do I do?
Change your password immediately via the "Forgot Password" flow (which also invalidates all active sessions). Then contact support so we can review recent account activity and secure your account.

Payments & Billing

What payment methods are accepted?
Zorexium accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Zorexium Gift Cards. Cryptocurrency payments are available on select listings.
Why was my payment declined?
Payment may be declined due to incorrect card details, insufficient funds, or bank security holds. Try a different payment method or contact your bank. Our systems never store full card numbers.
Is it safe to save my card on Zorexium?
Yes. Card data is tokenized via our PCI-compliant payment processor and never stored on Zorexium servers. You can manage saved payment methods in Account → Payment Methods at any time.
