Community Guidelines

Build a respectful and productive community

Our Community Values

The Zorexium Labs community is built on mutual respect, innovation, and collaboration. We're committed to creating a welcoming environment where all members can learn, share, and grow together.

Code of Conduct

All community members agree to:

  • Treat all members with respect and dignity
  • Avoid harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
  • Respect intellectual property and privacy
  • Share knowledge constructively and honestly
  • Accept feedback graciously and offer it thoughtfully
  • Report violations and help maintain community standards

Posting Guidelines

Be On-Topic

Keep discussions relevant to the forum category. Off-topic posts will be moved or removed.

Be Clear & Constructive

Ask clear questions, provide context, and offer solutions or suggestions when helping others.

Search Before Posting

Check if your question has been answered previously to avoid duplicate threads.

No Spam or Promotion

Don't post promotional content, affiliate links, or spam. Self-promotion is limited to designated areas.

Appropriate Language

Use professional language. Offensive language, slurs, and excessive profanity are not permitted.

Prohibited Content

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Harassment, threats, or bullying of any community member
  • Hate speech or discrimination based on any characteristic
  • Adult, violent, or otherwise inappropriate content
  • Piracy, copyright infringement, or illegal activity promotion
  • Malware, viruses, or phishing attempts
  • Personal information sharing (doxxing)
  • Spam and commercial advertisements

Reporting Violations

If you encounter content that violates these guidelines:

  • Click the "Report" button on the post or comment
  • Provide details about the violation
  • Our moderation team will review and take action
  • Don't engage with inappropriate content - reporting helps more than replying

Moderation Policy

Warnings

First violations typically result in a warning and comment removal.

Temporary Suspension

Repeated violations result in temporary account suspension (24 hours to 7 days).

Permanent Bans

Severe violations or continued disruptive behavior result in permanent removal from the community.

Appeals Process

If you believe a moderation action was unfair, you can appeal by contacting moderators@zorexium.labs with details of your appeal within 30 days.

Help Us Keep Community Safe

Everyone has a role in maintaining a positive community. Thank you for following these guidelines and helping us build a better Zorexium Labs experience.
