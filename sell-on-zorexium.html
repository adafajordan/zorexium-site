Seller Requirements

To keep Zorexium a trusted marketplace, all sellers must meet these requirements.

Identity Verification

Valid government-issued ID required. Business sellers must provide their business registration number.

Bank Account / Payout Method

A valid bank account is required to receive payouts from sales.

Authentic Hardware Only

All products must be genuine. Counterfeit, stolen, or prohibited items are strictly banned and will result in account termination.

Reliable Shipping

You must ship orders within 2 business days of purchase and provide valid tracking information to buyers.

Maintain Seller Rating

Sellers must maintain a minimum 3.5★ rating. Consistent low ratings may result in listing restrictions or account review.

Agree to Seller Guidelines

All sellers must read and agree to Zorexium's Seller Guidelines and Terms of Service.
