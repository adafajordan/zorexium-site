What's Covered

The Zorexium Marketplace Guarantee covers every transaction completed through our platform. Whether you're buying a used GPU, a full workstation, or precision peripherals, you're protected.

Item Not Received

Full refund if your order never arrives

Item Not as Described

Full refund if the listing was materially inaccurate

Significantly Damaged in Transit

Refund or replacement for transit damage

Counterfeit or Unauthorized Item

Full refund and seller suspension

Defective Hardware

Refund or exchange within 30 days of receipt

Wrong Item Shipped

Prepaid return label and correct item or refund

How It Works

Our guarantee works automatically. There's no separate enrollment required—every purchase on Zorexium is covered from the moment payment clears.

1

Browse & Buy

Find your hardware and complete checkout through our secure platform. Payment is held in escrow until delivery is confirmed.

2

Receive & Inspect

Your item ships from the seller. Upon delivery, you have 3 business days to inspect and confirm receipt—or report an issue.

3

Confirm or Dispute

Mark the order as received to release payment to the seller, or open a dispute if there's a problem. Our team reviews all disputes within 48 hours.

4

Resolution

If your claim is approved, you'll receive a full refund to your original payment method within 3–5 business days. In cases of seller negligence, Zorexium absorbs the loss.

Eligible Purchases

The Marketplace Guarantee applies to all purchases made through the Zorexium checkout system. Transactions conducted outside our platform (e.g., direct bank transfers arranged off-site) are not covered.

Covered Categories

  • Processors & CPUs
  • Graphics Cards & GPUs
  • Motherboards
  • Memory & RAM
  • Storage (SSD, HDD, NVMe)
  • Complete Systems & Builds
  • Peripherals & Accessories
  • Networking & Server Equipment

Requirements

  • Purchased via Zorexium checkout
  • Claim filed within 30 days of delivery
  • Account in good standing
  • Evidence provided (photos, tracking)
  • Attempt to resolve with seller first

Filing a Claim

To file a claim under the Marketplace Guarantee, follow these steps. Most claims are resolved within 3–5 business days.

  1. Step 1

    Contact the Seller

    Use the in-platform messaging system to notify the seller of the issue. Allow 48 hours for a response before escalating.

  2. Step 2

    Open a Dispute

    Navigate to Marketplace Settings → Claim History and click "Open New Claim." Select your order and describe the issue clearly.

  3. Step 3

    Provide Evidence

    Upload photos of the item, packaging, and any relevant documentation. Include tracking information and communication records with the seller.

  4. Step 4

    Await Review

    Our Trust & Safety team reviews your claim within 48 hours. They may request additional information from you or the seller.

  5. Step 5

    Resolution & Refund

    If your claim is approved, your refund is processed to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. You'll receive an email confirmation when the refund is issued.

Dispute Resolution

When buyers and sellers cannot reach an agreement, Zorexium mediates the dispute through our formal arbitration process. Our team is impartial and bases decisions on evidence submitted by both parties.

Mediation Phase (0–3 days)

Our team facilitates a structured conversation between buyer and seller to find a mutually agreeable resolution. Over 70% of disputes are resolved at this stage.

Adjudication Phase (3–7 days)

If mediation fails, our Trust & Safety team issues a binding decision based on the evidence. Both parties must comply with the decision.

Final Appeal (7–14 days)

Either party may appeal the adjudication decision once by submitting new evidence. Appeals are reviewed by a senior Trust & Safety specialist.

All dispute decisions are final after the appeal period expires. Zorexium reserves the right to take action against sellers who repeatedly generate disputes or act in bad faith.

Exclusions & Limitations

While our Marketplace Guarantee is broad, there are some scenarios where it does not apply or where coverage is limited.

Not Covered

  • Transactions completed outside the Zorexium platform
  • Claims filed more than 30 days after confirmed delivery
  • Buyer's remorse or change of mind (no-fault returns)
  • Damage caused by the buyer after receipt
  • Items where the listing accurately disclosed known defects
  • Digital goods, software licenses, or activation codes
  • Accounts found to be engaged in abuse of the guarantee system

Coverage Limits

  • Guarantee coverage is capped at the original transaction amount including shipping
  • Consequential damages (e.g., data loss, downtime costs) are not covered
  • Maximum of 3 guarantee claims per account per 12-month period
  • Enterprise orders over $10,000 are subject to separate terms

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to file a claim?
You must file a claim within 30 days of your item's confirmed delivery date. Claims submitted after this window are not eligible for the Marketplace Guarantee unless exceptional circumstances can be demonstrated.
What if the seller disputes my claim?
Both parties present evidence to our Trust & Safety team, who make a binding decision based on the facts. The process is impartial and both sides have an equal opportunity to be heard. We handle hundreds of disputes every month and are experienced at identifying fraudulent claims from either side.
How quickly will I receive my refund?
Once your claim is approved, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The time it takes for the refund to appear in your account depends on your payment provider—typically 1–3 additional business days for credit cards, and instant for PayPal.
Can I return an item if I simply changed my mind?
Change-of-mind returns are at the discretion of individual sellers. The Marketplace Guarantee covers issues with the item itself (defects, not as described, not received), not buyer's remorse. Check the seller's individual return policy before purchasing.
What happens if the seller is unresponsive?
If a seller does not respond to your message within 48 hours, you can immediately escalate to Zorexium support. Seller non-responsiveness is taken very seriously—persistent non-communication can result in the claim being decided in the buyer's favor and the seller account being restricted.
Is used / refurbished hardware covered?
Yes. Used and refurbished hardware is covered by the Marketplace Guarantee. If the item's condition was materially different from what was described in the listing, you are entitled to a refund. Listings must accurately represent condition grades (New, Like New, Very Good, Good, Acceptable).
