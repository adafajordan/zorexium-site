Getting Started Guide

Everything you need to begin your journey with Zorexium Labs

1. Create Your Account

• Visit the signup page and enter your email address

• Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)

• Verify your email by clicking the confirmation link

• Complete your profile information

2. Browse the Marketplace

Navigate to the Marketplace section to explore hardware listings. Use filters to narrow down products by:

  • Category (GPUs, CPUs, Networking, etc.)
  • Condition (New, Like New, Used)
  • Price range
  • Seller rating

3. Review Product Details

Click on any product to view:

  • Detailed specifications
  • High-quality images
  • Seller information and ratings
  • Customer reviews
  • Shipping information

4. Add Items to Cart

• Click the "Add to Cart" button on any product

• View your cart to review items and quantities

• Proceed to checkout when ready

5. Checkout & Payment

At checkout, you'll be able to:

  • Review your items and quantities
  • Enter shipping address
  • Select payment method
  • Review total cost including shipping and tax
  • Place your order

6. Track Your Order

After purchase, you'll receive:

  • Order confirmation email
  • Tracking number via email
  • Shipment updates
  • Estimated delivery date

7. Become a Seller (Optional)

Ready to sell? Visit the "Sell Hardware" section to:

  • Create a seller account
  • Set up your seller profile
  • List your first product using the Listing Wizard
  • Manage your inventory and orders

8. Join the Community

Connect with other enthusiasts in the Community Hub to:

  • Discuss hardware and technology
  • Share reviews and recommendations
  • Ask questions and get help
  • Participate in trading discussions
