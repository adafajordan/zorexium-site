Seller Guidelines

Best practices for success on Zorexium Labs

Seller Requirements

To maintain a high-quality marketplace, all sellers must:

  • Complete identity verification and provide valid business information
  • Maintain a professional seller account and storefront
  • Accept and comply with our Terms of Service and marketplace policies
  • Respond to customer inquiries within 24 hours
  • Maintain accurate inventory and product information
  • Ship orders promptly and use tracked shipping

Product Listings

Accurate Descriptions

Provide detailed, honest descriptions of your products. Include specifications, condition, and any defects or limitations.

Quality Images

Upload high-quality images from multiple angles. Clear photos help buyers make confident purchases and reduce returns.

Fair Pricing

Set competitive prices. We reserve the right to remove listings that are significantly overpriced.

Prohibited Items

Do not list counterfeit, stolen, or illegal items. Violators will be permanently banned.

Customer Service Standards

  • Respond to messages professionally and courteously
  • Honor stated shipping timelines
  • Work with customers to resolve issues fairly
  • Issue refunds for returns within 10 business days
  • Maintain a rating above 3.5 stars on average

Prohibited Practices

The following activities will result in account suspension or permanent ban:

  • Operating multiple seller accounts to bypass restrictions
  • Directing customers off-platform for transactions
  • Misrepresenting products or seller status
  • Manipulating reviews or ratings
  • Harassment or discrimination against customers
  • Price gouging or unfair pricing practices
  • Selling counterfeit or recalled items

Performance Metrics

We monitor the following to ensure marketplace quality:

Response Time < 24 hours
Average Rating > 3.5 stars
On-Time Delivery > 95%
Return Rate < 5%

Payment & Payouts

  • 5% marketplace commission on all sales
  • 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing fee
  • Payments issued weekly to verified bank account
  • Initial payout may take 1-2 weeks
  • Suspended accounts forfeit outstanding payments

Dispute Resolution

If a buyer files a dispute:

  • You'll have 7 days to respond with evidence
  • Our team will review and make a determination
  • Most disputes favor documented evidence (tracking, photos)
  • Repeated disputes can lead to account restrictions

Account Suspension & Termination

We may suspend or terminate seller accounts for:

  • Violating our Terms of Service or guidelines
  • Consistently poor customer service or ratings
  • Non-compliance with payment or verification requirements
  • Involvement in fraud or illegal activity
  • Excessive disputes or chargebacks

Suspended accounts may appeal within 30 days. Terminated accounts are permanent.
